✨ What Is Glass Skin and How Can You Get It?

“Glass skin” is a Korean skincare concept that describes skin so smooth, clear, and luminous that it looks like glass. It’s achieved by layering hydrating products, maintaining a consistent skincare routine, and focusing on achieving a naturally radiant, poreless look.

Why Is Glass Skin Trending?

The popularity of glass skin coincides with the global love for Korean skincare. People adore its youthful, hydrated, and healthy glow — perfect for those who prefer a minimal, no-makeup look.

Key Characteristics of Glass Skin

  • ✨ Smooth, poreless texture
  • 🌈 Even and bright skin tone
  • 💧 Hydrated, dewy glow

Consistency is key — using hydrating and balancing products regularly helps you achieve this effect.

5 Steps to Achieve Glass Skin

  • 1. Double Cleanse: Start with an oil cleanser, then follow with a gentle foam cleanser.
  • 2. Tone: Use exfoliating and hydrating toners for smoother texture.
  • 3. Serums: Apply one for brightening (Vitamin C) and one for hydration (Hyaluronic Acid).
  • 4. Moisturize: Lock in all the hydration with a nourishing cream.
  • 5. Sunscreen (AM) or Sleep Mask (PM): Protect during the day and hydrate deeply at night.

Tips for Different Skin Types

  • Oily Skin: Lightweight, non-comedogenic moisturizers work best.
  • Dry Skin: Try rich creams or slugging for extra moisture.
  • Sensitive Skin: Stick to fragrance-free, gentle hydrators.
  • Blemish-Prone Skin: Use non-clogging serums and spot treatments.
  • Combination Skin: Hydrate dry areas, mattify oily ones.
